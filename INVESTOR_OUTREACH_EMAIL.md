## Email Template

### The Problem We're Solving

Today, patients make critical healthcare decisions in the dark. They can't easily:
- Share their healthcare journeys and learn from others' experiences
- Find and verify the right healthcare providers for their specific needs
- Access transparent reviews and authentic stories from real patients
- Connect with medical professionals for guidance before committing to expensive treatments

This information asymmetry costs patients billions in ineffective treatments and unnecessary procedures, while quality healthcare providers struggle to reach patients who need them most.

### Our Solution: Healdox

Healdox is a comprehensive healthcare social platform that empowers patients to share their journeys, connect with verified medical professionals, and make informed healthcare decisions. Think of it as LinkedIn meets Healthgrades, with the community engagement of Reddit.

**Key Features:**
- **Patient Journey Sharing**: Users document and share their healthcare experiences, creating a global knowledge base
- **Verified Provider Network**: Board-certified physicians, nurses, and healthcare facilities with transparent reviews
- **Symptom Discussion Forums**: Community-driven support for specific conditions and symptoms
- **Direct Consultations**: Integrated appointment booking and telemedicine capabilities
- **AI-Powered Search**: Advanced healthcare provider discovery based on specialty, location, and patient reviews
- **Medical Records Management**: Secure, patient-controlled health data with granular access permissions

### Market Opportunity

- **Total Addressable Market**: $12B+ (U.S. healthcare consumer engagement and telehealth)
- **Target Markets**:
  - Patients seeking transparency and community support
  - Healthcare providers looking to build their practice and reputation
  - Pharmaceutical companies conducting market research and patient outreach

- **Revenue Model**:
  - Freemium subscription tiers for patients, providers, and pharma companies
  - Professional tier: $15-25/month
  - Enterprise tier: $50-100/month
  - Consultation booking fees (15-20% transaction fee)
  - Anonymous data insights for pharmaceutical research

## Email Best Practices

**Timing:**
- Send Tuesday-Thursday, 8-10 AM in investor's timezone
- Avoid Mondays (inbox overload) and Fridays (weekend mode)

**Follow-up Strategy:**
1. First follow-up: 3-4 days after initial email
2. Second follow-up: 1 week after first follow-up
3. Final follow-up: 2 weeks after second follow-up

**Personalization Checklist:**
- [ ] Research investor's recent investments
- [ ] Reference specific portfolio companies or thesis areas
- [ ] Mention mutual connections if applicable
- [ ] Cite their blog posts, tweets, or conference talks
- [ ] Explain why they specifically are the right fit

**Attachments:**
- Pitch deck (PDF, max 15 slides)
- One-pager (optional, for busy investors)
- Demo video link (2-3 minutes max)

## Key Metrics Investors Want to See

Prepare to discuss:
- **User Growth**: DAU, MAU, registration trends
- **Engagement**: Session duration, frequency, retention (D1, D7, D30)
- **Monetization**: ARPU, LTV, CAC, LTV:CAC ratio
- **Provider Network**: Number of verified providers, specialties covered, geographic distribution
- **Unit Economics**: Contribution margin per user/provider
- **Market Validation**: NPS score, testimonials, case studies
- **Competitive Position**: Feature comparisons, market share estimates
